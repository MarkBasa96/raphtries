Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Who we are

Raph Tries is a small content brand that posts short videos about gadgets, AI tools and everyday finds on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok. "Raph Tries Poster" is a private tool its owner uses to publish those videos.

What the tool does

Raph Tries Poster uploads videos made by Raph Tries to Raph Tries' own accounts: the Raph Tries Facebook Page, the @raphtries Instagram account and the @raphtries YouTube channel. It is used only by the owner of those accounts. It does not sign in other people, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.

Information we use

  • Our own sign-in tokens. To post, the tool holds access tokens for Raph Tries' own Google (YouTube) and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) accounts. They are stored only on the owner's computer and are used for nothing except uploading Raph Tries videos and their captions.
  • Our own videos. Before posting, a video may be sent to an AI service (Google Gemini and OpenAI) to help write its caption. These are Raph Tries' own videos, not anyone else's content.

We do not collect personal information from visitors to this website or from people who watch our videos. This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

Google user data

The tool uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the Raph Tries YouTube channel. Raph Tries Poster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Uploads are made under the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. Content is shared only with the platforms we post to, and with the AI caption services described above.

Removing access

Access for the tool can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access page and from Meta's Business settings. Doing so deletes nothing that has already been posted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @raphtries on Instagram.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
